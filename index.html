Bio

I am an assistant professsor in Department of Computer Science and Software Engineering at Auburn University. I received my Ph.D. degree from The Pennsylvania State University in 2025, where I was advised by Prof. Fenglong Ma. My research interests include machine learning, healthcare informatics, foundation models, and large language models (LLMs). More about my research can be found in the Group Page. Prior to that, I obtained my M.S. from University of Georgia and B.E. from Zhejiang University.

[Recruitment] The opportunities of Ph.D. students and research interns can be found in the Group Page.
